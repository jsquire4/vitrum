# App ideas — post-100% showcase / product candidates

> Captured 2026-06-12 from a capability brainstorm. Back-pocket list — none of
> these are scheduled. Each idea is grounded in capabilities that exist (or land
> with the road-to-100 campaign); the "leans on" line names the load-bearing
> vitrum features so feasibility stays checkable against the ledger.
>
> Common thread: competitors demo "rays in a browser." Vitrum is infrastructure —
> contract-honest, gate-verified, with **state you can save** (GI export/import),
> **gradients you can descend** (inverse/adjoint), and **spectra you can trust**
> (hero-wavelength). The apps fall out of those three.

## 1. Stained-glass studio (the white whale, productized)

Design a window in-browser with real glass physics: spectral dispersion through
cathedral glass, thin-film iridescence on flashed layers, Beer-Lambert depth
tinting, MNEE caustics pooling on a stone floor. Killer feature: scrub
time-of-day with the Preetham sun and watch the caustic pattern migrate across
the nave over a simulated day — before a single piece of glass is cut.
Restorers mock replacement panels against photographed originals.

**Leans on:** spectral PT, thin-film TMM, MNEE, attenuation/Beer-Lambert,
Preetham sky + scene-lighting sun position, stained-glass-extensions,
progressive walkaround→PT handoff.

## 2. Material capture from a photograph

Inverse session + path-replay adjoint pointed at a photo of real glass, fabric,
or car paint → recover material parameters by gradient descent through the
renderer, in a browser tab. No Mitsuba install, no Python env. "What glass
recipe is this 13th-century window?" as a web app.

**Leans on:** `createInverseSession`, path-replay adjoint (V24), captureFrame.

## 3. Instant-on photoreal scenes via shipped light fields

Converge DDGI/ReSTIR/PPG state once — server-side on lavapipe, headless
(already proven by the gate infra) — and ship the baked radiance with the
scene as a CDN asset. Visitor gets fully-lit GI on frame one; PT refines
underneath via progressive handoff. Console-quality "baked but dynamic"
lighting as a static asset.

**Leans on:** GI-state export/import (v4), headless deno+lavapipe pipeline,
createProgressiveEngine.

## 4. Configurators that close sales (jewelry first)

Diamonds are meaningless without spectral dispersion — RGB renderers fake the
fire. Walkaround GI while the customer rotates the ring; converged spectral PT
with real fire the moment they pause. Also: watch dials (thin-film), car paint
(clearcoat), titanium anodization (iridescence ranges).

**Leans on:** progressive handoff, spectral dispersion (Abbe/Cauchy),
clearcoat/iridescence lobes, glTF pipeline + backend planner.

## 5. Interactive light-transport textbook

PT, BDPT, SPPM, ReSTIR-PT, photon mapping as composable, oracle-verified
integrators behind one scene contract. Veach's thesis as a website: same scene,
four tabs; watch BDPT eat the difficult-light case; toggle MIS off and watch
variance explode. The oracle suite makes the demos provably correct, not vibes.
Best-in-class teaching artifact for rendering courses.

**Leans on:** multi-integrator pt-webgpu, the oracle.*.test.ts estimator
transcriptions (reusable as in-page explainers), behavioral gate scenes.

## 6. Browser-tab render farm

`seedAccumulator` + `captureFrame` make accumulation state portable → N tabs
each converge a tile or sample range; a coordinator merges. Classroom or
community crowd-renders a film frame.

**Leans on:** accumulator seed API, captureFrame pixel readback, deterministic
RNG streams.

## 7. Ground-truth synthetic data factory (headless)

The deno+lavapipe gate pipeline doubles as a dataset factory: physically
correct images with exact AOVs (normals, depth, albedo, per-primitive IDs via
pickPrimitive) for training denoisers, segmentation models, NeRF/3DGS
baselines. Spectrally correct, license-clean, generated by CI.

**Leans on:** headless WebGPU path, aux buffers, pickPrimitive, captureFrame,
glTF ingestion at scale.

## 8. Daylighting analysis for real architecture

glTF building in, physically-based sky, scrub a date slider: museum
conservators checking annual lux exposure on a tapestry; architects checking
February glare on the reception desk. Fidelity exists; this is a UI away.

**Leans on:** Preetham sky + sun position by date/time, glTF pipeline,
walkaround for interactive scrubbing + PT for the report renders, captureFrame
for quantitative lux extraction (linear readback).

# Tier 2 — unlocked by the SOTA wave + browser hardware RT

> Captured 2026-06-12. Premise: the road-to-100 forward-looking items (F1–F6 in
> `plan/road-to-100.md`) land AND WebGPU gains a hardware ray-tracing extension
> (RT cores). Those multiply: ~10–50× ray throughput × 2–4× LDS convergence ×
> CWBVH × wavefront × shipped denoiser. **The phase change: ground-truth light
> transport crosses under the 16ms interaction threshold.** Tier 1 ideas are
> single-player, mostly static, patience-mediated; Tier 2 is live, multiplayer,
> measurement-grade — the three adjectives the current envelope forbids. When
> truth is interactive, the progressive handoff becomes a fallback, not the
> architecture.

## T2-1. Path-traced games in a tab

RTX-class visuals (ReSTIR + 1–2spp + denoiser at 60fps), no install — a URL.
Host-owns-lifecycle contract lets existing web game engines adopt vitrum as a
renderer without surrendering their loop.
**Needs:** hardware RT, F1/F2/F3/F4, F6 (unbiased default).

## T2-2. Path-traced WebXR

2×90Hz stereo — only exists with RT cores. Museum walkthroughs where stained
glass disperses as you move your head; clients walking unbuilt buildings under
a physically-correct February sky. Spectral PT in VR exists on no platform —
browser-first would be shocking.
**Needs:** hardware RT, F4 wavefront, foveated/ReSTIR budget tricks.

## T2-3. Live differentiable rendering (webcam material capture)

The inverse session's gradient loop at interactive rates: webcam feed in,
material parameters converging live as you tilt the object. AR material
scanning, paint matching, gemstone authentication by optical signature.
**Needs:** hardware RT, adjoint at frame rate; builds on Tier-1 idea 2.

## T2-4. Figma-for-3D with ground-truth lighting

Multiplayer scene editing where every participant sees converged GI while
dragging. GI-state export/import becomes the multiplayer sync primitive —
peers share radiance, not just geometry.
**Needs:** hardware RT, F6; GI-state v4 as the wire format.

## T2-5. Sensor-grade digital twins

Live data feeds driving a photoreal twin whose lighting is quantitatively
correct (linear captureFrame = lux meters anywhere in the scene). Factory
lighting compliance, greenhouse PAR, solar-gain dashboards — rendered
measurement, continuous, in a tab.
**Needs:** hardware RT for continuous re-convergence; spectral for photometry.

## T2-6. Volumetric weather/fire, live

F5 heterogeneous volumes are transformative only at interactive rates:
cloud-resolving weather viz, wildfire smoke dispersion with correct
scattering, flight-sim atmospherics. Null-collision tracking needs exactly the
ray budget RT cores provide.
**Needs:** hardware RT + F5 together.

## T2-7. Optical & lighting engineering CAD

Interactive caustic design: drag a lens surface, watch the focal caustic move,
converged. IES-accurate luminaire design, headlight beam compliance,
light-pipe routing — desktop optical-simulation seats cost five figures; this
is a tab with spectral truth.
**Needs:** hardware RT, MNEE/SPPM at interactive rates, spectral.

## T2-8. Relightable captured reality

Neural capture (splats/NeRF) fused with PT at speed: phone-scan a room,
relight it with ground-truth transport. NRC at full hardware strength bridges
captured radiance and simulated lighting.
**Needs:** hardware RT, NRC maturity (A6 done), a splat/NeRF ingestion seam
(new contract work — the one Tier-2 item needing core API additions).
